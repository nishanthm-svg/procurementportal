Our Story

A Passion for Craft
& Community

Maison Noir was born from a simple belief: that exceptional food and drink, served in a space that feels like home, can transform an ordinary afternoon into something extraordinary.

Our chefs source locally, cook seasonally, and present each dish with the care and precision of a painting. From the first espresso of the morning to the last glass of wine at night, we are here for every chapter of your day.

Why Guests Love Us

The Maison Noir Experience

☕

Specialty Coffee

Our baristas are championship-trained. Every cup is a ritual — from bean selection to the final pour, we treat coffee as a craft.

🌿

Farm to Table

We partner with local farms and change our menu seasonally. If it isn't fresh, it isn't on the plate. Simple as that.

🍷

Curated Wine List

Our sommelier selects natural and biodynamic wines from small-batch producers around the world to complement every dish.

🕯

Intimate Atmosphere

Thoughtfully designed lighting, warm textures, and unhurried service create a space where time slows down beautifully.

🎂

Private Events

Celebrate milestones with us. Our private dining room seats up to 30 guests with bespoke menus and dedicated service.

🌅

All-Day Dining

Open from early morning through late evening. Breakfast, brunch, lunch, afternoon tea, dinner — we have your whole day covered.
