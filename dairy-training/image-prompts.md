# Dairy Farm Training — Gemini image prompts

Source: NDDB "Small Holder Dairy Farm Management Guideline" (64 pages).
Audience: smallholder farmers in Andhra Pradesh, many unable to read. Each picture must teach on its own.

## Style block (prepended to every prompt)

> Simple, warm, flat-colour educational illustration for rural Indian dairy farmers who cannot read.
> Setting: a small village dairy in Andhra Pradesh, India. People: a friendly Telugu woman farmer in a cotton saree, or a man in lungi and shirt.
> Animals: realistic Indian cows (crossbred and desi) and Murrah buffaloes.
> One clear action, large subjects, plain soft background, bold outlines, bright natural colours.
> ABSOLUTELY NO words, letters, numbers or captions anywhere in the image. Landscape 4:3.

Do/Don't images use a **big green tick ✔** on the correct side and a **big red cross ✘** on the wrong side (symbols, not text).

## Prompts (file name → prompt)

### Lesson 1 — Know your farm's health (key indicators)
- `01-healthy-farm.png` — A happy woman farmer standing beside her clean cattle shed with a healthy cow, a buffalo, a young heifer and a small calf; a steel milk can full of milk in front.
- `01-calf-every-year.png` — A circular year-wheel drawn as a ring of 12 small sun-and-moon icons around a cow nursing a newborn calf, showing "one calf every year". Only icons, no numbers.

### Lesson 2 — Heat signs and the right time for AI
- `02-heat-signs.png` — A cow in heat standing still while another cow mounts her from behind in an open village yard; the standing cow has a raised tail and clear stringy mucus from the vulva; she is bellowing.
- `02-ai-timing.png` — Split panel: left side shows an evening sky with a cow showing heat and the farmer noticing; right side shows the next morning sunrise with a trained AI technician with a portable frozen semen container (liquid-nitrogen can) arriving on a motorbike at the farmer's door.

### Lesson 3 — Newborn calf care
- `03-calf-breathing.png` — Farmer gently cleaning mucus from a newborn calf's nose and mouth with clean cloth, then rubbing the calf's chest with dry straw; the calf sits upright on its chest.
- `03-cow-licking.png` — Mother cow licking her wet newborn calf clean on fresh dry straw bedding.
- `03-navel-care.png` — Close-up of hands tying the newborn calf's navel cord with clean thread and dipping it in a small bottle of brown iodine.
- `03-colostrum.png` — Woman farmer feeding thick yellow first milk (colostrum) from a bottle to a newborn calf, with a wall clock nearby showing the calf was born just a short while ago.

### Lesson 4 — Growing calves healthy
- `04-ors.png` — Farmer preparing oral rehydration drink for a weak calf: a 1-litre bottle of clean water, a small heap of glucose powder, baking soda and salt with teaspoons, then giving it to the calf.
- `04-calf-deworm-tag.png` — A veterinarian giving deworming medicine by mouth to a young calf that has a yellow ear tag.
- `04-calf-starter.png` — A two-month-old calf eating calf-starter grain mix and fresh green grass from a clean small trough.

### Lesson 5 — Mastitis and clean milking
- `05-clean-udder.png` — Woman washing a cow's udder with clean water and drying it with a fresh clean towel before milking; a pile of clean towels beside her.
- `05-cmt-test.png` — Hands holding a white four-cup mastitis test paddle with milk from each teat; one cup shows thick gel-like milk (problem), others normal.
- `05-teat-dip.png` — Dipping each teat in a small cup of antiseptic teat dip right after milking.
- `05-stand-after-milking.png` — After milking, the cow stands eating fresh green fodder at the manger instead of lying on the floor; floor is clean and dry.
- `05-dirty-vs-clean.png` — Do/Don't split: right side ✘ dirty wet muddy shed with a cow lying in dung and a dirty towel; left side ✔ clean dry shed, clean towel, cow standing.

### Lesson 6 — After-calving problems and body condition
- `06-milk-fever.png` — A cow lying down after calving, unable to stand, with its neck turned to one side toward its flank; a worried farmer calling the veterinarian on a mobile phone.
- `06-bcs.png` — Three cows side by side: very thin cow with visible ribs and hip bones, a well-rounded healthy cow, and an overweight fat cow; a green tick above the middle cow only.

### Lesson 7 — Dangerous diseases: know the signs
- `07-fmd.png` — Cow with foot-and-mouth disease: blisters on tongue and lips, thick ropy saliva dripping from mouth, limping on a sore foot.
- `07-lsd.png` — Cow with lumpy skin disease: many round raised lumps all over the skin, flies around.
- `07-hs.png` — Buffalo with swollen throat and neck, drooling, breathing with difficulty.
- `07-ticks.png` — Close-up of ticks on a cow's skin near the udder and ear, and a farmer spraying the shed for tick control.
- `07-isolate-call-vet.png` — Sick cow kept in a separate pen away from the healthy herd, with its own bucket; farmer calling the veterinarian on phone.

### Lesson 8 — Vaccination and deworming
- `08-vaccination-camp.png` — Village vaccination camp: a government veterinarian vaccinating a cow in the neck, a line of farmers with cows and buffaloes waiting under a tree.
- `08-cold-chain.png` — Vaccine vials kept inside an ice box with ice packs, carried carefully by the veterinary worker.
- `08-deworm.png` — Farmer giving a deworming bolus by mouth to an adult cow, with help from the vet.

### Lesson 9 — Abortion and keeping disease out (biosecurity)
- `09-safe-disposal.png` — Farmer wearing rubber gloves and gumboots burying aborted material in a deep pit and sprinkling white lime powder over it.
- `09-quarantine.png` — Newly bought cow kept in a separate quarantine shed far from the main herd, a calendar on the wall indicating about a month of waiting (icons only).
- `09-farm-gate.png` — Farm entry with a foot-dip tray of disinfectant, a hand-washing tap with soap, and a visitor washing hands before entering the shed.

### Lesson 10 — Hoof care
- `10-hoof-trim.png` — A trained person trimming the hoof of a cow restrained safely in a hoof-trimming crate.
- `10-foot-bath.png` — Cows walking one by one through a shallow foot-bath at the shed entrance on a clean dry floor.

### Lesson 11 — Strength of our desi breeds
- `11-desi-breeds.png` — Proud group of Indian native breeds under hot sun: white Ongole bull-humped cow, red Gir cow with long curled ears, red Sahiwal cow, and a black Murrah buffalo cooling in a pond.

### Lesson 12 — Feeding the right way
- `12-feed-groups.png` — Shed divided into sections: milking cows eating rich green fodder and concentrate, a pregnant cow, a dry cow, heifers, and calves each in their own area.
- `12-green-fodder.png` — Farmer cutting fresh green fodder with a chaff cutter: maize/sorghum grass and leafy cowpea/lucerne in separate heaps.
- `12-mineral-water.png` — Cow drinking from a large trough of clean water; a small bowl of mineral mixture powder added to its feed.
- `12-silage.png` — Making silage: chopped green maize packed tightly into a pit, pressed by feet, then covered airtight with a black plastic sheet and weights; inset shows golden-yellow good silage.
- `12-mouldy-feed.png` — Do/Don't split: ✘ mouldy black-green fungus-covered feed and wet hay; ✔ fresh dry feed stored on a raised platform in sacks, away from rats.

### Lesson 13 — Protect animals from summer heat
- `13-heat-stress.png` — Do/Don't split: ✘ cow panting with open mouth and drooling under harsh sun with no shade; ✔ cows resting comfortably under a shaded shed with a fan and water sprinklers, fresh water trough.

### Lesson 14 — Milk cost and farm records
- `14-records.png` — Woman farmer writing daily milk amounts in a notebook register in the cattle shed; a smartphone showing a dairy app next to her.
- `14-milk-cost.png` — Visual of money going out (feed sacks, green fodder, medicine, electricity bulb, labour) and money coming in (milk cans to the Shreeja milk collection centre) balanced on a weighing scale.
